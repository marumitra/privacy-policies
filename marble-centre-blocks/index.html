Privacy Policy — Marble Centre Blocks

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Marble Centre Blocks ("the App") is an internal operations tool built for Marble Centre (marblecentre.co.in) to track marble/granite blocks through their processing lifecycle — from arrival to cutting, dispatch, and sale. The App is developed and maintained by MaruMitra Tech Ventures Private Limited on Marble Centre's behalf.

The App is not available to the general public. It is intended solely for use by authorized Marble Centre staff and administrators, who are provided login credentials directly by Marble Centre. There is no public sign-up.

Information We Collect

Account information — provided by Marble Centre when a staff account is created:

  • Username and password (stored securely; passwords are never stored in plain text)
  • Display name and role (e.g. supervisor, admin)

Operational business data — entered by staff while using the App:

  • Block records: weight, dimensions, cutting/dispatch status, and related processing history
  • Photos of blocks, cut slabs, and packing lists, taken with the device camera or selected from the photo library
  • Names and contact details of suppliers and buyers (parties) entered by staff as part of normal record-keeping
  • Machine and equipment records used internally for tracking which machine processed a block

This data belongs to Marble Centre's business operations — it is not collected for advertising, analytics profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the business.

Permissions the App Requests

  • Camera — to photograph blocks, slabs, and packing lists at each stage.
  • Photo library — to attach an existing photo instead of taking a new one.

No other device permissions (location, contacts, microphone, etc.) are requested.

How Data Is Stored

App data is stored using Supabase, a database and storage infrastructure provider, hosted in the ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region. Photos are stored in a private storage bucket and are only ever made accessible through short-lived, signed links generated at the moment they're needed — they are not publicly listed or browsable.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase (our infrastructure provider), solely to store and serve the data described above.

The App does not currently use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data Retention & Deletion

Business records (block/dispatch history) are retained for as long as Marble Centre requires them for ongoing operations and record-keeping. A staff member's account and associated login credentials are deactivated when they leave Marble Centre. To request deletion of specific personal data associated with a staff account, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The App is an internal workplace tool for adult staff and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children.

Changes to This Policy

If how the App collects or uses data changes, this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will reflect the most recent revision.
